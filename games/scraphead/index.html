Wave incoming

The Swarm has landed.

Every run is a fresh fight across an alien-infested junkyard. Fight, loot, upgrade, repeat. See how far a pile of scrap with legs can get.

Blast the Swarm

Survive 30-second waves of skittering, spitting, exploding aliens. Your guns fire on their own. You aim, you dodge, you panic.

Loot everything

Hoover up scrap, shop at the Scrapyard, and merge matching weapons into higher ranks. Stack trinkets until the numbers get silly.

Topple the big ones

Elites, robot bounty hunters and five towering bosses stand between you and a saved planet. They do not want to be toppled.

Zone 1

Pick your path

Every zone is a branching route map. Choose your fights, dodge the ones you can't win (yet), and meet the boss at the end.

  1. Battle

    Survive the wave. Scoop up scrap and XP.

  2. Unknown

    Could be a chest. Could be a curse. Spin the wheel.

  3. Scrapyard

    Buy, sell and merge guns at the merchant.

  4. Elite

    A much bigger alien with a much better reward.

  5. Bounty Hunter

    A robot gunslinger wants your head. Win it, keep their gun.

  6. Boss

    End the zone. Or it ends you.
