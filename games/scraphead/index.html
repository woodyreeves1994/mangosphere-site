Wave incoming

The Swarm has landed.

Every run is a fresh fight across an alien-infested junkyard. Fight, loot, upgrade, repeat. See how far a pile of scrap with legs can get.

Blast the Swarm

Wave after wave of skittering, spitting, exploding aliens. Stay moving, keep shooting, try not to panic.

Loot everything

Grab every bit of scrap, hit the shop between fights, and build a loadout that gets gloriously out of hand.

Topple the big ones

Elites, bounty hunters and towering bosses stand between you and a saved planet. Who, or what, they are is classified.

Zone 1

Pick your path

Every zone is a branching route map. Choose your fights, dodge the ones you can't win (yet), and meet the boss at the end.

  1. Battle

    Fight off the Swarm and grab the scrap.

  2. Unknown

    Could be a chest. Could be a curse. Spin the wheel.

  3. Scrapyard

    Spend your hard-earned scrap.

  4. Elite

    A much bigger alien with a much better reward.

  5. Bounty Hunter

    A robot gunslinger wants your head. Win it, keep their gun.

  6. Boss

    End the zone. Or it ends you.
