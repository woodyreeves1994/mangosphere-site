The studio

Hi! We’re Mangosphere.

We’re a small independent studio with a big soft spot for games you can’t stop replaying. We care about crunchy feedback, readable chaos, and characters you’d happily put on a T-shirt.

No filler, no committees. Just us, making the games we want to play and sharing them with you.

Juicy by default

Every hit, pickup and explosion should feel great. If it doesn’t squish, it isn’t done.

Chunky & clear

Bold outlines and strong silhouettes, so you always know what’s trying to eat you.

Better together

We design with co-op in mind. Chaos is always better with a friend.

One more go

Runs that play out differently every time, so there’s always a reason to jump back in.
