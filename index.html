About us

Hi! We’re Mangosphere.

I’m a dad from the UK who’s been playing games for as long as I can remember. Mangosphere is where I finally started making them.

We’re a real indie studio. No big publisher, no focus groups, just real people who love games, building the kind we’d want to play ourselves.
